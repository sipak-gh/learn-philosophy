# Appendix B — Timeline

The history of philosophy from Thales and the Upanishads to today, with the chapter that covers each event. Ancient dates are approximate, and several (especially for early Indian and Chinese thinkers) are traditional or disputed; works are dated by first publication unless noted. Previous: [Appendix A — Glossary](appendix-a-glossary.md). Next: [Appendix C — Reading List](appendix-c-reading-list.md).
