# Readings

Every public-domain book the course asks you to read is in this folder: 148 books, all from [Project Gutenberg](https://www.gutenberg.org/). 147 are EPUBs; open them with any EPUB reader (Apple Books, Calibre, KOReader, Thorium, Foliate, a Kobo or most other e-readers). Wittgenstein's *Tractatus* is a PDF, because that is the only format Gutenberg offers for it. The chapters give exact sections to read. Books you must procure yourself (modern translations of Aristotle's *Metaphysics* and the Chinese classics, Wittgenstein's *Philosophical Investigations*, and most twentieth-century and contemporary philosophy, from Heidegger, Sartre and Beauvoir to Rawls, Nozick and Nagel) are listed in each chapter's Required reading block and in [Appendix C](../appendix-c-reading-list.md).
